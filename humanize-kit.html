Questions

Can't I just prompt ChatGPT to capture my tone?

You can, and it gets you partway for one chat. The kit is the difference between that and a voice that holds up everywhere, every time. It's a saved file you keep and reuse. A prompt lives in one chat, then it's gone. It only learns from your real, AI-free writing (most people feed it AI-tidied text and capture the machine instead). It's built through a testing loop until it actually sounds like you. And it gives you a score, so you know it worked.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. If you can paste text into Claude or ChatGPT, you can run this. The guide walks you through setup, and you can ask Claude to install it for you in plain English.

Is it a subscription?

No. It's £29, once. You own the files.

Does it work in ChatGPT, or only Claude?

It's built and tested for Claude Code, where it runs best. It also works in Codex and the Claude app, which both support skills, and in ChatGPT on Team and Enterprise, where ChatGPT supports skills. On a personal or Plus ChatGPT account, skills aren't available yet, so use Claude or Codex for now. There's a short setup file for each.

Do I need a paid AI plan?

The rewrite works on any plan. The score runs a small script, so it needs a tool that can run code, which is a paid Claude or ChatGPT feature. For the full experience, including the score, use a paid plan.

What if I don't have writing samples?

It still takes the AI out and gets you to clean, plain English. For the "sounds like you" part, you give it a few real samples, even voice notes work. Two minutes and you've got your voice file.

What exactly do I get?

The two skill folders, the worked examples, and the setup & use guide as a PDF. Everything in one download.
